Executor loader

Connect your Roblox session

Runs the bridge connector so Vyre can see and drive your game.

Open your executor while you are in a Roblox game. Any executor with loadstring + HttpGet works.

Paste this into the editor and execute it (or drop it in your autoexec folder):

loadstring(game:HttpGet("http://localhost:16384/script.luau"))()

Your client appears here within a second. Pick it from the client selector and you are live.

Port 16384 has no auth — keep it on localhost or a trusted LAN. For a different host set getgenv().BridgeURL before the loader.
